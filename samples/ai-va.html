PORTFOLIO SAMPLE · SIMULATED CLIENT WORK

AI + Technical VA Workflow

A fictional work sample demonstrating how I would organize research, documentation, task triage, and AI-assisted quality control for a remote client.

Scenario

A small remote team needs help organizing competitor research, preparing a short manager summary, cleaning an information tracker, and drafting a reusable SOP.

Workflow

01

Research

Collect relevant information from multiple sources, compare findings, and record useful details in a structured tracker.

02

Organize

Remove duplicates, standardize labels, flag missing information, and create a short summary of the most important findings.

03

Review

Use AI to speed up drafting and categorization, then manually review the output for factual errors, unclear wording, and formatting issues.

Example Deliverables

Research tracker: company name, website, service category, pricing notes, notable strengths, observed gaps, and source links.

Manager summary: concise overview of patterns, key competitors, possible opportunities, and items that need human follow-up.

SOP: repeatable instructions describing how future research should be collected, checked, named, stored, and reported.

Tools

ChatGPT · Google Docs · Google Sheets · Browser Research · Manual Quality Review
