VELO

THE PROBLEM

Why this exists.

A runner must feel readable and fair while still rewarding timing and experimentation. VELO investigates movement, collision readability, visual effects and environment transitions.

Key modules

  • Bedroom and study-inspired visual environments
  • Coin collecting and variable road shapes
  • Character skins, power-shop UI and visual iteration
MY ROLE & THE AI PROCESS

Built with direction and review.

My role: Game design decisions, implementation direction, AI-assisted debugging, hands-on playtesting and iteration management.

Where AI helped: AI-assisted coding and targeted investigation of gameplay, collisions, road mechanics and visual issues.

Evidence & validation
