OUR STORY

A neighborhood café, imagined with intention.

Harbor Bean is a fictional business created as a front-end portfolio demonstration. The goal is to show how a small local brand can feel premium without becoming complicated or heavy.

The experience focuses on clear hierarchy, mobile responsiveness, strong calls to action, readable content, subtle interaction, and a visual identity that feels consistent from the first screen to the contact form.

01 · RESPONSIVEDesigned to adapt cleanly from desktop to phone.
02 · ACCESSIBLEReadable contrast, semantic sections and motion preferences.
03 · INTERACTIVEMobile navigation, scroll reveals and working form feedback.
04 · LIGHTWEIGHTNo framework or heavy library required.
