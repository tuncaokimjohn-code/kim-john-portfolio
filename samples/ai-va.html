SIMULATED CLIENT WORK · AI + VA

From scattered requests to structured work.

A fictional work sample showing how I would organize remote research, documentation, and AI-assisted administrative tasks. This is not paid client work.

Scenario

A small remote team needs competitor research, a manager-ready summary, a cleaned tracker, and a repeatable SOP.

STEP 01 / RESEARCH

Collect and cite sources

Gather current source URLs, record dates and service categories, and flag uncertainty rather than guessing missing data.

STEP 02 / ORGANIZE

Make the tracker usable

Deduplicate rows, standardize service categories, keep link provenance, and prepare a summary organized by important findings.

STEP 03 / VERIFY

Human review of AI output

AI may assist with grouping and drafting. Verify claims against original sources, check formatting and completeness, and clearly mark follow-up questions.

Example deliverables

Research tracker: provider, category, price/source, date checked, evidence link, strengths and open questions.

Manager summary: patterns, observations, actionable next steps and unresolved issues.

Procedure: repeatable research, naming, checking and reporting instructions.

This page demonstrates process structure, not fabricated completed research data.
